# Vehicle Return and Cancellation Policy

## Online reservation
A customer may cancel an online reservation within 48 hours if no purchase contract has been signed. The reservation fee is refunded to the original payment method within five business days.

## Signed purchase contract
A signed purchase contract is generally binding. A general 14-day return right does not automatically apply to a vehicle bought at a physical dealership. Statutory rights for defects remain unaffected.

## Distance contract
When the complete purchase is legally concluded as a distance contract, the customer may have a statutory withdrawal right. The exact applicability depends on the sales process and contract terms. The customer should consult the contract or contact support.

## Damaged or misrepresented vehicle
Suspected defects or materially incorrect listing information must be reported to the dealer promptly. The marketplace does not decide legal claims and does not guarantee a refund.
