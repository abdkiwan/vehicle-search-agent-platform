# Documents Needed When Buying a Vehicle

Buyers should normally bring a valid identity document, their driving licence when a test drive is planned, and proof of payment or financing approval.

For registration, commonly required documents include the vehicle registration certificate parts I and II, proof of insurance using the electronic insurance confirmation number, proof of the latest roadworthiness inspection, and a SEPA mandate for vehicle tax.

Requirements can differ by registration authority. Customers should verify the current requirements with their local authority.
