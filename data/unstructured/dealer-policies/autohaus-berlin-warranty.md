# AutoHaus Berlin Used-Vehicle Warranty

AutoHaus Berlin includes a 24-month used-vehicle warranty on eligible vehicles priced at or above EUR 15,000. The warranty covers specified mechanical and electrical components subject to the written warranty conditions.

Wear parts, cosmetic defects, damage caused by misuse, and faults disclosed in the purchase contract are excluded. Claims must be reported before repair work begins. The warranty does not replace statutory defect rights.
