# GreenDrive Hamburg Battery and Charging Policy

GreenDrive Hamburg provides the available manufacturer battery-warranty information for each electric vehicle. When a recent battery health report exists, it is attached to the listing or provided before contract signature.

A displayed range value is based on manufacturer or vehicle data and is not a guarantee of real-world range. Eligible vehicles include a 24-month dealer warranty; battery degradation within manufacturer tolerance is not treated as a dealer-warranty defect.
